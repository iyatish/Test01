Every blog starts somewhere. This one starts here.

I've been meaning to write more for a long time. Not to build an audience, not to establish a brand — just to think more clearly. Writing does something to an idea that thinking alone doesn't: it forces precision.

## What I'll write about

Mostly software. I spend a lot of time thinking about how systems are built, how they break, and how they could be simpler. I'll write about patterns I find useful, tools I reach for, and things I wish I'd understood earlier.

I'll also write about adjacent topics — productivity, mental models, and the occasional detour into whatever I'm reading.

## What to expect

Posts will be short when they should be short and long when they need to be. No fillers, no recaps of things you already know.

Thanks for reading.
